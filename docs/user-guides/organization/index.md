# Organization

These guides cover what an organization admin does outside the product pages: the settings and records that span every project.

| Guide                               | You end up with                                                                                         |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Read the audit log](audit-log.md)  | The user, time and origin of any change made in your organization, and a retention that fits your policy |

## Before you start

- Select your organization in the top bar. The **Audit log** and **Settings** entries at the bottom of the sidenav act on that organization.
- Membership of the predefined **Admin** group, or of a group that carries the matching permission set. The sidenav hides the entries you cannot open.

For the concepts behind these pages, read [Audit Log](../../features/audit-log.md).
